About Us

Wayback to Deci is a community-driven server dedicated to recreating the authentic, classic Decimation experience. After the official Decimation servers shut down, many projects attempted to emerge and be the new playable servers, but all of them ended in a short time and none captured the old vibes. As veteran players who experienced those servers firsthand, we decided to bring back the gameplay and atmosphere we all miss.


What we are currently working on:
Rebuilding Bukovka: We are carefully rebuilding the official Bukovka map using videos and photos to ensure total accuracy. Since the project is currently in development, the server is not public yet. Consider joining our Discord server to get updated!

Disclaimer: This is an independent community project and is not affiliated with BoehMod, Mojang, or Microsoft. We are only using the Decimation mod for the server.

Joining Building Team

Requirements to join the building team:

  • It is nice if you are good at building.
  • You must be good at rebuilding precisely from videos/screenshots, etc... since we are rebuilding the map the most precise way possible (and trust me it isn't easy).
  • You must have Premium Minecraft (Java).
  • You must be reliable.
  • It is really nice if you have played the official servers a lot.
  • We probably won't accept people totally new to Decimation, however if you are really good at the previous requirements you may be accepted as well.

If you are interested, contact me via DMs on Discord (paperocolui)!
