About us

This is a community server of the Minecraft mod called Decimation. Our goal is to bring back old authentic Decimation experience in the best way possible replicating it including rebuilding the official Bukovka map used in the official servers of the map, we are currently working on rebuilding it from videos and photos, so the project has not been published yet, to get updated consider joining our Discord Server. We started this project because we miss official Decimation experience, so we decided to replicate it, we are people that played that servers and we know how they were. After Decimation Official Servers shutdown many project born but every project has ended and didn't have old Decimation vibes, and here we arrive, to get the classical experience back. The name of the project, Wayback to Deci, can describe our project, we want to go back to classical Decimation, so Wayback, and the short name of Decimation: Deci. This is a community server and we are not linked to BoehMod.

Not affiliated with Mojang or the Decimation mod team BoehMod.
